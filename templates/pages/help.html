Answers to common questions. If you can't find what you need, contact us.

Booking

Why does a car show as "Booked"?

It is already booked for part of the dates you picked. Other dates may be free: open the car and check its availability calendar, or use the suggested next free dates.

How long is a car held while I book?

We hold the car for {{ hold_minutes }} minutes while you finish checkout. If you don't pay in that time, the car becomes available to others again.

Can I change my dates?

Before paying, go back to the dates step and pick new dates; your driver details are kept. After paying, cancel the booking and book again, or contact us.

Driving licence

What do I need to give you?

The licence number, the name printed on it, the date of birth and the expiry date. Nothing to upload. We check the number format, the issuing state, the expiry date and that the driver is 18 or older.

Can someone else drive the car?

Yes. On the driver step choose "Someone else will drive" and enter that person's licence details. They must bring the original licence when the car is handed over.

My licence was not accepted. Why?

The message on the form says which part failed — usually the number format, an expiry date before the end of the trip, or an age under 18. Fix it and try again.

Payment and cancellation

How do I pay?

Online by UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay. The total shown includes {{ TAX_PERCENT }}% tax.

How do I cancel, and will I get a refund?

Open My Bookings and choose Cancel. Cancellation is free any time before pickup, and the full amount is refunded to your original payment method.

Pickup and delivery

How much is home delivery?

Home delivery costs ₹{{ DELIVERY_CHARGE }}. The car is returned to its pickup location.

What should I bring at pickup?

Your original driving license. Please return the car with the same fuel level it had at pickup.
