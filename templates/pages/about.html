DriveGo is a self-drive car rental service in Kolkata. You choose a car, pick your dates, upload your documents and pay online. Then you collect the car from one of our locations or have it delivered to your door.

What we offer

  • {{ fleet_count }} cars, from small hatchbacks to 7-seater SUVs and luxury sedans.
  • {{ location_count }} pickup location{{ location_count|pluralize }} across the city.
  • Live availability, so you only see cars that are free for your dates.
  • The full price, including taxes, before you pay.
  • Free cancellation any time before pickup.

How booking works

  1. Search for a car and choose your pickup and return dates.
  2. Upload your driving license and a government ID.
  3. Choose store pickup or home delivery.
  4. Pay securely. We confirm the booking once your documents are checked.
