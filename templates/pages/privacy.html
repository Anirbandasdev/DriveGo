This policy explains what information DriveGo collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and profile photo from your sign-in (handled by Clerk).
  • Booking details: the car, dates, pickup or delivery address and any instructions you add.
  • Documents: the driving license and government ID you upload.
  • Payment details: payment status and reference numbers. Card and bank details are handled by Razorpay and never reach us.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your bookings.
  • To check your identity and driving license before handing over a car.
  • To process payments and refunds.
  • To contact you about your booking.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Your documents are visible only to our verification staff.
  • We share information with the services we need to run bookings: Clerk (sign-in), Razorpay (payments) and Supabase (secure storage).
  • We may share information if required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information. Some booking and payment records may need to be kept for legal and accounting purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@drivego.com or see our contact page.
